Dr. Dimas Danar Dewa

Urban Systems & Spatial Modeling

Modeling Urban–Environment Dynamics for Climate-Resilient and Sustainable Development

I am a Research Fellow at the School of Architecture, Planning and Policy Development (SAPPD), Institut Teknologi Bandung, Indonesia. My research integrates GIScience, remote sensing, and advanced spatial modeling to investigate urban transformation, ecological conditions, climate risks, and spatial inequality.

I develop data-driven approaches to understand urban–environment interactions and support climate-resilient, low-carbon, and sustainable development, particularly in rapidly transforming cities of emerging economies.
